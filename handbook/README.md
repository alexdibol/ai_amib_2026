# Executive handbook — What a CEO Needs to Learn About AI

[Read the executive handbook (PDF)](The%20indispensible%20AI%20Handbook%20for%20CEO.pdf)

**Alejandro Reynoso · From Models to Autonomous Institutions**

The handbook provides the continuous written account of the six-part programme. Read it after the [speech](../speech/Discurso_AMIB_DEFINITIVO.pdf) to connect the executive argument to the operating and organisational choices behind a company's AI business plan.

Its central question is what an institution can responsibly delegate. Six chapters develop model literacy, the governed operating cycle, agentic organisation, delegated authority, bounded adaptation, and the Fortress and AI Factory. A recurring research-to-decision service connects capability to evidence, controls and ownership. Three lecture syntheses and a final synthesis bring the ideas together; a resource register and references support further study.
